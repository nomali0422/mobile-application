Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enquire about an adventure?

Complete the contact form and prepare an email with your enquiry, or contact the organisation using its confirmed contact details.

Where can I check activity prices?

Visit the Fees & Booking page to review the example prices and estimate your group costs.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Availability and arrangements must be confirmed by the organisation.
